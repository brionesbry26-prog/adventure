Data Privacy and Security Statement

Prepared for the Local Government Units of Romblon

Adventure is designed to follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC). This statement summarizes how the app handles personal data.

1. Data we collect

DataPurpose
Name, email, profile photoTo create and secure the user's account
Saved places, trips, bucket listTo plan and save the user's trips
Photos the user uploadsFor memories, albums and trip recaps
Location (only when allowed)To show nearby places and record visits
Reports and suggested updatesTo keep tourism information accurate

2. How we protect it

  • Hosted on Google Firebase with encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Strict access rules: users can only read and change their own private data.
  • Admin powers run only through server functions that check admin rights; admin sessions log out when idle.
  • An audit log records every admin change: who, what and when.
  • Photos and community reports are moderated before they are shown publicly.

3. What we share

We never sell personal data. The LGU receives aggregate, non-identifiable statistics only (for example, "120 visits to this place this month"). Personal data is shared only when required by law, or under a separate Data Sharing Agreement.

4. Rights of users

Users can access and correct their data in the app, object to processing, and request deletion of their account and data through Help & support or by email. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days. Users may also file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.

5. Retention

Data is kept while the account is active. When an account is deleted, the user's profile, trips, photos and saved items are deleted. Some records may be kept longer only where the law requires it.
