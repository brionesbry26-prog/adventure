II. Rationale

Romblon has many natural and cultural attractions: white-sand beaches, islands, waterfalls, heritage churches and forts, and the marble industry. Yet tourists find it hard to plan a visit. Information about destinations, accommodations and contacts is scattered across social media pages and blogs and is often outdated. Travel between islands depends on ferry and boat schedules that change and are hard to find before reaching the port. Many destinations have weak or no mobile signal, so online maps and guides stop working when tourists need them most. As a result, visitors tend to go to the same few places, while other municipalities and small local businesses receive little exposure.

A single, reliable digital guide, with information verified by the LGU, can make Romblon easier to visit, spread tourists across more municipalities, and support the province's tourism and digital transformation goals.

III. Objectives

General objective

To promote tourism in the Province of Romblon through a mobile travel guide app that helps tourists discover, plan and travel the province, using information verified by the LGU.

Specific objectives

  1. To provide one source of accurate information on tourist destinations in all 17 municipalities;
  2. To help tourists plan complete trips quickly through an AI trip planner that prioritizes destinations chosen by the LGU;
  3. To make ferry and transport schedules easier to find, with community reports on port status;
  4. To allow tourists to travel with offline maps in areas without mobile signal;
  5. To give the Tourism Office a dashboard to manage information and see aggregate tourism trends; and
  6. To increase the visibility of lesser-known destinations and local businesses.

IV. Project description

Adventure is a free Android mobile application (iOS planned) with a web-based admin dashboard. It is already working and available as a beta version tested on real devices.

ComponentDescription
Place discoveryTourist spots by municipality and type, with photos, descriptions, best time to visit, and Must visit, Featured and Trending badges.
Isla, AI trip plannerBuilds a day-by-day itinerary (up to 6 stops a day) from the tourist's interests, using only places in the verified database and prioritizing places highlighted by the LGU.
Map itineraryEvery stop on a map with suggested length of stay and distance and travel time between stops.
Ferry and transportRoutes, operators, fares, weekly schedules and the next departure; tourists can report port status.
Offline mapsTrips and maps can be saved before travel and used without signal.
Bucket list, memories and recapSaved places, photo albums, trip recaps and shareable cards for social media.
Events, weather, expensesLocal events and festivals, daily weather, and a trip expense tracker.
Admin dashboardFor authorized Tourism Office staff: manage places and highlights, review community updates and photos, maintain ferry routes, view usage statistics and a log of every change.

V. Scope and limitations

  • The pilot covers [municipality/ies]; province-wide rollout follows after evaluation.
  • Ferry times are estimated departure times (ETD) from operators and are labeled as such; they do not replace official advisories.
  • The AI planner suggests itineraries only; tourists remain responsible for their own travel decisions.
  • The app is available on Android first. iOS is planned for [target date].
  • The app does not process payments or bookings.

X. Data privacy and security

Adventure is designed to follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). It collects only the data needed for the app to work, never sells personal data, shares only aggregate statistics with the LGU, and lets users request deletion of their account and data. The system runs on Google Firebase with encrypted connections, strict access rules, server-checked admin functions, idle logout for admins and an audit log of every admin action. See the attached Data Privacy Statement.
