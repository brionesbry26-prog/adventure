Presenter's Guide and Checklist

How to prepare, present and follow up with the LGU

1. The papers, in order

#PaperWhen and howCopies
1Letter of IntentSend first, 1–2 weeks before. Bring to the Tourism Office, get a "received" stamp on your copy.2 (signed)
2Project ProposalAttach to the letter, and bring printed copies to the presentation.5–10
3Presentation (slides)Shown during the meeting. Bring on a laptop and USB; download as PDF in case.USB + laptop
4Draft MOUHand over at the end, for their legal office to review.2–3
5Data Privacy StatementAttach to the proposal; officials often ask about data.5–10
6Developer Profile + valid ID copyAttach to the letter of intent.2
7Proof of statusSchool endorsement letter (if student) or DTI/SEC, BIR and Mayor's permit (if business).2

Requirements differ per office. Call the Tourism Office first and ask: "What documents do you need for a courtesy call and a partnership proposal?" Put everything in a long brown envelope or clear folder, one set per official.

2. Before the meeting

Letter of intent received, meeting date confirmed by phone

Read the LGU's tourism plan, flagship spots and upcoming festivals

That town's places are in the app with good photos and descriptions

Demo on two phones, logged in, a trip already planned and saved offline

Laptop with the slides, the admin dashboard logged in, the video ad, an HDMI adapter

Practiced the full talk in under 20 minutes

Business casual attire; arrive 15 minutes early

TimeSlidesWhat to do

4. Expected questions

QuestionSuggested answer
How much will it cost us?[Your terms.] Free for tourists; we ask for support and data, not funding.
Who updates the information?The Tourism Office through the dashboard, with tourists' suggestions reviewed before going live.
Is users' data safe?We follow RA 10173, collect minimal data, never sell it, and share only counts with the LGU.
No signal on the islands?Trips and maps can be saved offline. (Show it.)
Are ferry times accurate?They're labelled estimates; with LGU help we get official schedules, plus live port reports.
iPhone?Android first; iOS planned [date].
What if you stop the project?The MOU returns LGU data and removes LGU logos; continuity terms can be added.
Can the AI give wrong places?Isla uses only our verified places database and puts LGU priorities first.

If you don't know an answer, say: "I'll confirm that and send it in writing within the week."

5. After the meeting

Thank-you letter within 3 days, with answers you promised

Write down attendees, concerns and agreed next steps

Follow up on the MOU review after 1–2 weeks

After signing: endorsement or Sanggunian resolution, DPO and privacy documents, permission to use LGU logos
