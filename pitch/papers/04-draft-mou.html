DRAFT FOR DISCUSSION · SUBJECT TO REVIEW BY THE LGU LEGAL OFFICE

MEMORANDUM OF UNDERSTANDING

Partnership for the Adventure Travel Guide App

KNOW ALL MEN BY THESE PRESENTS:

This Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) is entered into this ___ day of ________, 20__, at [place], Romblon, by and between:

The PROVINCIAL GOVERNMENT OF ROMBLON / MUNICIPALITY OF ______, a local government unit with office address at [address], represented by HON. [FULL NAME], Governor / Municipal Mayor, hereinafter referred to as the "LGU";

— and —

ADVENTURE, [a sole proprietorship registered with DTI / an individual developer], with address at [address], represented by [FULL NAME], Developer, hereinafter referred to as "ADVENTURE".

WITNESSETH

WHEREAS, the LGU is mandated to promote tourism and the general welfare of its constituents under the Local Government Code of 1991 (RA 7160);

WHEREAS, ADVENTURE has developed a mobile travel guide application and an admin dashboard designed to promote tourism in the Province of Romblon;

WHEREAS, both parties wish to work together to give tourists accurate information and to promote the destinations and businesses of Romblon;

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

Article I. Purpose

This MOU sets the terms of cooperation between the LGU and ADVENTURE for the pilot implementation and promotion of the Adventure app in [municipality/ies].

Article II. Responsibilities of the LGU

  1. Endorse the Adventure app as a partner travel guide for [the Province / Municipality];
  2. Provide official tourism information, such as accredited destinations, establishments, events and fees;
  3. Designate a focal person from the Tourism Office to review and update information through the admin dashboard;
  4. Help promote the app through official LGU channels and allow QR code posters at ports, offices and tourist spots; and
  5. Help coordinate with port authorities and operators for accurate transport schedules.

Article III. Responsibilities of ADVENTURE

  1. Operate, maintain and host the app and admin dashboard, and keep it free for tourists;
  2. Give dashboard accounts and training to authorized LGU staff;
  3. Correct information promptly upon request of the LGU;
  4. Submit monthly usage reports containing aggregate data only, and a pilot evaluation report; and
  5. Protect personal data in accordance with Article IV.

Article IV. Data privacy

Both parties shall comply with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173), its implementing rules and NPC issuances. ADVENTURE shall not sell or share users' personal data with the LGU or any third party except as required by law. Reports to the LGU shall contain aggregate, non-identifiable data only. Should any sharing of personal data become necessary, the parties shall first execute a separate Data Sharing Agreement.

Article V. Intellectual property and use of names

The Adventure app, its source code and design remain the property of ADVENTURE. Information, logos and photos supplied by the LGU remain the property of the LGU, which grants ADVENTURE a non-exclusive license to use them within the app and agreed promotional materials for the term of this MOU.

Article VI. Financial terms

[No financial obligation shall arise for either party under this MOU. Each party shall bear its own costs.]

Article VII. Term, renewal and termination

This MOU takes effect upon signing and remains in force for [one (1) year], renewable by mutual written agreement. Either party may terminate it with [thirty (30)] days' written notice. Upon termination, ADVENTURE shall, at the LGU's request, return or delete information supplied by the LGU and remove LGU logos from the app.

Article VIII. Amendments and disputes

Amendments shall be made in writing and signed by both parties. Disputes shall be settled amicably through dialogue between the parties.
