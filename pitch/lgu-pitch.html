1. Executive summary

Adventure is a mobile travel app made for Romblon. It helps tourists discover places across the province, plan a complete trip with an AI assistant named Isla, check ferry schedules, find their way even without signal, and keep memories of their visit.

For the LGU, Adventure is a free digital channel to promote Romblon: official tourist spots, local businesses, events, and transport information in one place, managed through a web dashboard and kept fresh with the help of the community.

"One app where every tourist can discover, plan, and travel Romblon — with information the LGU can trust."

Section 2

The challenge

Romblon has beautiful islands, beaches, falls and heritage sites, but tourists find it hard to plan a visit.

Scattered information

Tourist spots, resorts and contacts are spread across Facebook pages, blogs and word of mouth. Much of it is outdated.

Hard-to-find ferry schedules

Trips between islands depend on ferry and boat schedules that change, and tourists often learn about them only at the port.

Weak signal on the islands

Many destinations have little or no mobile data, so online maps and guides stop working when travelers need them most.

Lesser-known towns are missed

Visitors cluster in a few known spots. Other municipalities and small local businesses get little exposure.

Section 3

The solution: Adventure

A free Android app (iOS planned) that guides a tourist from the first idea of a trip until the memories after it.

Step of the tripWhat Adventure does
DiscoverBrowse places by town and type, with Must visit, Featured and Trending badges, photos, descriptions and the best time to go.
PlanChat with Isla, the AI trip planner. Pick interests (beach, falls, heritage, food…) and get a day-by-day itinerary of up to 6 stops a day.
TravelFerry and boat schedules with the next departure, a route map with distance and travel time between stops, offline maps and weather per day.
RememberPhoto albums pinned to places, a trip recap on a map, and a shareable trip card that promotes Romblon on social media.

Section 3 · continued

Main features

Built and tested on real devices. Already available as a beta version.

1

Isla — AI trip planner

Tourists tell Isla their interests and travel dates. Isla builds a complete itinerary that puts the province's must-visit and featured places first.

2

Interactive map itinerary

Every stop is a numbered pin on a full-screen map, with the best time to visit, suggested length of stay, and km plus drive or walk time from the last stop.

3

Ferry and transport schedules

Routes, operators, fares and weekly schedules, with the next departure in large clear text. Travelers can report port status (e.g. delayed, cancelled) to warn others.

4

Offline maps

A trip can be saved for offline use before leaving signal coverage, so maps and the itinerary keep working on the islands.

5

Bucket list and favorites

Tourists save places they want to visit, then build a full trip from their list in one tap. Points reward saving and visiting places.

6

Nearby and travel footprints

See places close to you with walking or driving time, and a personal map of every place visited in Romblon.

7

Memories and trip recap

Photo albums by town and place, a recap of each trip on a map, and a share card for Facebook and other social media.

8

Events, weather and expenses

Local events and festivals, the weather for each trip day, and a simple tracker for trip expenses.

Tourism admin dashboard

A secure web dashboard where authorized tourism staff can manage the information tourists see in the app.

Manage places

Add and edit tourist spots, photos, descriptions, best time to visit and contact details.

Highlights board

Choose which places are Must visit, Featured or Trending across the province.

Community updates

Tourists suggest corrections (closed, new fees, changed hours). Staff review and approve them before they go live.

Maintain routes and schedules and see port status reports from travelers.

Section 6

Data privacy and security

Adventure is designed to follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and the rules of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

How we protect users

  • Minimal data. Only what the app needs: account name and email, saved places, trips and photos the user chooses to upload.
  • User control. Users can view and correct their data, and request deletion of their account and data through Help & support or email (completed within 30 days). A Privacy Policy and Terms of Use explain this in plain language.
  • No selling of data. Personal data is never sold. Reports to the LGU use aggregate counts only (e.g. "120 visits to this place this month").
  • Secure cloud. Hosted on Google Firebase with encrypted connections and strict access rules on every record. App verification to block fake clients is being enabled.
  • Admin protection. Admin powers run only through checked server functions. Admin sessions log out when idle, and every admin action is recorded in an audit log.
  • Content moderation. Photos and community reports are reviewed before they are shown publicly.

Partnership proposal

We are asking the LGU for support, not funding. These are the specific requests:

1

Official endorsement

A letter of endorsement or a Sanggunian resolution recognizing Adventure as a partner travel guide app for Romblon.

2

Official tourism data

Access to the list of accredited tourist spots, resorts and establishments, event calendars and official fees.

3

A focal person

One contact in the Tourism Office to review place information and approve updates through the dashboard.

4

Promotion

Sharing the app on official LGU pages, and QR code posters at ports, tourism offices and major tourist spots.

5

Transport coordination

Help in getting accurate ferry schedules from port authorities and shipping operators.

6

Pilot area

Permission to run a pilot in one or more municipalities and gather feedback from tourists and the office.

What Adventure provides

  • The app, free for tourists to download and use.
  • Dashboard accounts and training for tourism office staff.
  • Technical maintenance, updates and hosting [proposed terms, e.g. at no cost during the pilot].
  • Regular reports on app usage (aggregate, no personal data).
  • QR code posters and promotional materials for LGU use.

Section 9 · For the presenter

How to present to the LGU

Before the meeting

  • Send the letter of intent to the Tourism Office and ask for a schedule (courtesy call). Follow up by phone after 3–5 working days.
  • Learn the LGU's tourism priorities: their tourism development plan, flagship destinations and upcoming festivals. Mention them in your talk.
  • Make sure that town's places are in the app with good photos before you present.
  • Prepare the demo on two phones, logged in, with a trip already planned and saved offline. Do not depend on venue Wi-Fi or signal.
  • Print 5–10 copies of this proposal and bring a USB copy of the slides and the video ad.
  • Wear business casual. Arrive 15 minutes early.

Suggested flow (20 minutes)

TimePartWhat to say or show
1 minGreetingThank the officials, introduce yourself and the purpose of the visit.
2 minThe problemScattered info, ferry schedules, weak signal. Use a real local example.
1 minVideo adPlay the short Adventure video.
7 minLive demoTheir town's places → ask Isla to plan a trip → map itinerary → ferry schedule → offline mode → admin dashboard.
3 minBenefits and privacyWhat the LGU gains (Section 4) and how users' data is protected (Section 6).
2 minThe askEndorsement, data, focal person, pilot (Section 7). Be specific.
4 minQ&AAnswer clearly. If unsure, promise to follow up in writing.

After the meeting

  • Send a thank-you letter within 3 days with the answers you promised and the draft MOU.
  • Note who attended, their concerns and the agreed next steps.
