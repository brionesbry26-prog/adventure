About the beta install

Adventure is currently Android-only and still in beta, so it isn't listed on Google Play yet. Because of that, Play Protect may flag the installer with a warning — this is normal for early beta APKs distributed outside the Play Store, not a sign the app is unsafe.

As with any beta, you may run into occasional bugs; Adventure doesn't collect data it doesn't need, and your privacy is protected by design.
