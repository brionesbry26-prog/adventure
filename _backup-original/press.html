Summary

About Adventure

Adventure is a mobile travel companion built specifically for Romblon Province, Philippines. Users pick their islands and the app builds an itinerary around real ferry routes, terminals, and places. Itineraries generated while online can be viewed again offline, which helps where inter-island signal is weak. The app also includes Isla, an in-app AI trip assistant for questions about any municipality, fiesta, or place across Romblon. After a trip, users mark their stops as done, upload their memories, and create their own travel journal, with every visited place pinned on a personal travel map. Adventure is developed independently by Bryan Ronnie Briones, in coordination with local organizations across Romblon, with a long-term vision of expanding province by province toward a unified Philippine tourism and inter-island ferry navigation platform.
