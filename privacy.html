Privacy Policy.

What the Adventure app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

In short

  • We collect what the app needs to work: your account, your trips and saved places, and what you choose to share.
  • Your location is used only while you use Nearby or the map, and it isn't stored on our servers.
  • We don't sell your data and don't show third-party ads.
  • You can turn off notifications or location at any time, and ask us to delete your account and data.

1. Who we are

Adventure is a travel guide app for Romblon, Philippines. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. It follows the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

2. Information you give us

  • Account: your name, email address, profile photo, and how you sign in (Google or email)
  • Profile and preferences: the interests you choose, travel preferences and settings
  • Trips: destinations, dates, itineraries, expenses, notes, and the friends you invite or join with a trip code
  • Saved items: favorites, bucket list, followed ferry routes and places you mark as visited
  • Photos and memories you upload
  • Messages: what you ask Isla, support tickets, feedback, place reports and app ratings

3. Information collected automatically

  • Device: a push-notification token, used to deliver notifications
  • Crash reports: when the app crashes or hits an error, technical details (device model, app version, the error and your account id) are sent so we can fix it
  • Security: hashed records of sign-ins and code requests, used to stop spam and abuse
  • Location: only if you allow it and use Nearby or the travel map, to show places around you. It stays on your phone and isn't saved to your account
  • Data on your phone: cached places, schedules and map tiles for offline use, and settings such as whether you've seen the feature tour

4. How we use it

  • To run your account and keep your trips, saved places and memories in sync across devices in real time
  • To build itineraries from your interests and dates, and to personalise Home
  • To answer your questions in Isla
  • To send the notifications you allow: trip reminders, ferry route alerts, weather, events and replies from our team
  • To keep the app safe: rate limits, word filters, tier limits and blocking abuse
  • To read your reports, feedback and ratings, and improve the app

5. Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions and Cloud Messaging) stores and syncs your data and sends notifications
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose it
  • Google Gemini answers Isla's questions through our own server. Your question and the trip details needed to answer it are sent; your email isn't
  • OpenWeatherMap provides forecasts for your trip days through our own server
  • OpenStreetMap provides the map tiles and place search (what you type in a place search is sent to it), and OSRM the road routes
  • Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports (device model, app version, what went wrong and your account id) so we can fix problems

Each provider handles data under its own privacy terms. We don't sell your information and don't use advertising trackers.

6. Who can see your information

  • Only you can see your private trips, saved places, expenses and settings
  • Members of a shared trip can see that trip and the photos added to it
  • Our admin team sees support tickets, feedback, place reports and ratings in order to respond, and may see account details when handling abuse
  • We may disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety

7. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections and is protected by access rules, so each account can only read and change what it should. One-time codes and reset tokens are stored hashed and expire quickly. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in private.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When your account is deleted, your profile, trips, photos, saved items and tokens are deleted, and trips you shared pass to another member or are removed. Security records expire automatically. Some records may be kept longer where the law requires it.

9. Delete your account

You can ask us to delete your Adventure account and its data at any time:

  • In the app: Profile → Help & support → send a message asking to delete your account
  • By email: write to adventureromblon@yahoo.com from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my account"

We confirm the request and delete the account within 30 days. This removes your profile, trips, photos, memories, saved items, expenses, ratings, support messages, reports and notification tokens. Trips you shared pass to another member or are removed. Records the law requires us to keep are kept only as long as it requires.

10. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit your profile and preferences, and turn off notifications or location at any time. Under the Data Privacy Act you can also ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to how it's used: contact us below. You can also complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

11. Children

Adventure isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we'll delete it.

12. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app about important changes.

13. Contact

Questions or requests about your data: use Help & support in the app, or email adventureromblon@yahoo.com.

See also our Terms of Service.
