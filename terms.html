Terms of Service.

The rules for using the Adventure app, in plain words.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

In short

  • Adventure is a free travel guide to Romblon: places, events, ferries, trip plans and Isla, our AI travel assistant.
  • Schedules, ETDs, prices and advisories can change — always confirm ferry trips with the operator before you travel.
  • Be respectful: no false reports, spam, offensive words, or photos you don't have the right to share.
  • You can stop using Adventure and ask us to delete your account at any time.

1. Accepting these terms

By creating an account or using Adventure as a guest, you agree to these Terms of Service and to our Privacy Policy. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

2. What Adventure does

Adventure helps you discover and plan travel around the islands of Romblon, Philippines:

  • Browse tourist spots, beaches, food, places to stay, and events and fiestas by island and town
  • Check ferry routes and schedules with estimated times of departure (ETD), fares and Live Status advisories
  • Plan trips: choose your islands, travel dates and interests, and get a day-by-day itinerary you can edit and share
  • Ask Isla, the in-app AI travel assistant, about places, ferries and trip ideas
  • Save favorites and a bucket list, mark places visited, keep photo memories, track trip expenses and see your travel footprints map
  • Earn points and badges, invite friends to a trip with a code, and get trip, ferry and weather reminders
  • Report outdated place information, send feedback and rate the app

3. Travel information is a guide

Ferry schedules and ETDs are estimates. Trips can be delayed or cancelled because of weather, the Coast Guard or the operator, and prices, opening hours and place details can change. Isla's answers and generated itineraries are suggestions and can be wrong. Always check with the ferry operator, the place or local authorities before you travel. Adventure doesn't sell tickets or take bookings, and isn't responsible for services provided by ferry operators, hotels, restaurants or guides.

4. Accounts and guest mode

You can sign in with Google or with your email address, verified with a one-time code. Keep your sign-in private: you're responsible for activity on your account. To protect everyone, sign-ins, code requests and some actions are rate-limited. As a guest you can browse places, events and ferry schedules; saving, planning, posting and Isla need a free account.

5. Tiers and rewards

Each account has a tier (Wanderer, Explorer or Trailblazer) that sets limits such as saved favorites, bucket-list items, followed routes, photos per place and daily Isla messages. Points and badges are earned by using the app, for example by visiting places and completing trips. Points have no cash value and can't be transferred. We may change tiers, limits and rewards; the current ones are always shown in the app.

6. Your content

You keep ownership of the photos, notes, reports, ratings and messages you add. You give Adventure a free, non-exclusive licence to store them and show them inside the app to the people they're meant for, such as members of a shared trip, or our team for support tickets and place reports. Only upload photos you took or have the right to share, and don't include other people's private information.

7. Acceptable use

Please don't:

  • Post offensive, hateful or abusive words, spam or gibberish (the app filters these)
  • Send false place reports, fake ratings or misleading feedback
  • Harass other travellers, or misuse trip invites and referral codes
  • Try to break, overload or scrape the app, or get around its limits and security
  • Use the app for anything illegal

We may remove content, limit features or suspend accounts that break these rules.

8. Notifications

With your permission, we send push notifications about your trips, followed ferry routes, weather on trip days, events, replies from our team and new places. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

11. Ending your use

You can stop using Adventure at any time and ask us to delete your account through Help & support or by email (see how to delete your account). We may suspend or close accounts that break these terms or put other users at risk.

12. Changes

We may update these terms as the app changes. We'll update the date above and tell you in the app about important changes. Using Adventure after an update means you accept the new terms.
